# Beers
* **Red Hat Enterprise Lager (RHEL)**:  A rock-solid, ultra-stable brew with a 10-year shelf life guarantee and zero unexpected flavor changes.
* **Ansible Amber**: A perfectly automated brew that tastes exactly the same every single time you pour it, regardless of your glass or temperature.
* **The Upstream IPA** : A heavy-hitting, double dry-hopped brew where the "source" hops are added first to ensure every subsequent batch in the ecosystem maintains maximum flavor integrity.
